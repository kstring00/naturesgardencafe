# 5-minute user test (v3)

Run with 3 people who have never seen the site. Phone only. Don't help. Write down what they say out loud.

## Setup
- Text them the link, the way a customer would get it.
- Ask them to think out loud the whole time.

## Task 1 (read it word for word)
> "You're at home in League City and hungry. Figure out what this place is, whether they'd deliver to you, what a milanesa is, and start an order."

## Task 2 (new in v3)
> "Find out how far they deliver without reading the FAQ."

Pass: they scroll to the map, read a ring label ("about 8 miles") or the town labels, and answer in under 30 seconds.
Fail: they open the FAQ, or they can't say a number.

## What to watch for
| Moment | Pass looks like | If it fails, note their exact words |
|---|---|---|
| First 5 seconds | "Argentinian food / empanadas / delivery", before scrolling | |
| "What is this place?" | They say café inside a health store, not a vitamin shop | |
| Walk-in scroll | They notice the room change and keep reading, not confused by the pin | |
| "What's a milanesa?" | They swipe or scroll the pamphlet and read the one-line description back | |
| Delivery decision | They find the rings on the map and decide yes for League City | If they hesitate: confirm the area with Marina |
| Starting the order | They tap **Order delivery** (not scrolling for a form) | |
| Order panel | They understand to call, and don't feel tricked | |
| Sample captions | Nobody thinks the photos are the real café | |

## Three questions after
1. "What would you have ordered, and why?"
2. "Was there anything you wanted to know before ordering that you couldn't find?"
3. "Would you use this instead of calling? Why or why not?"

## Scoring
- 3 of 3 tap Order delivery within 60 seconds: ship it.
- Anyone asks "how long?" or "is there a fee?": get those from Marina before launch.
- Anyone fails Task 2: make the ring labels bigger before anything else.
