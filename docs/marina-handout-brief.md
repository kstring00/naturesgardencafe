# Brief: a printable handout for Marina

Give this file to Claude and ask for a PDF. Everything below is what the site needs from her; nothing else.

## Purpose
A 4-page, letter-size, printable worksheet Marina can fill in by hand (or read from while Kyle types).
Warm, simple, big type, lots of white space to write in. Her café's colors: cream paper, olive green, sky blue, gold.
Title: "Nature's Garden Cafe & Resto: your story, in your words." Subtitle: "Everything the website still needs. Takes about 20 minutes."

## Page 1: Your story (write it the way you'd say it to a regular)
Three lined boxes, each with a prompt and a one-line example of the *kind* of thing we mean (not sample copy to reuse):
1. **How the café came to be.** Where you're from, how you started cooking here, why Friendswood. Two to four sentences.
2. **What makes it Argentine, here.** The dishes, the way you cook, what people from home say when they eat here.
3. **The daily board.** How the soups and salads change. Is there a rhythm (Monday soup, Friday special)?

## Page 2: Three empanadas, three stories
One row per filling, two boxes each:
- **Beef**: "Tell us about this one" + "One practical detail" (e.g. how it travels, how many people order a dozen, is it the classic)
- **Chicken**: same
- **Spinach**: same, and note that it's vegetarian
Footnote: a sentence or two each is plenty. We print your words as they are.

## Page 3: The facts (check boxes and short blanks)
- **Hours** for each day: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun, with open and close blanks. Same as the market? ☐ yes ☐ no
- **Delivery area**: we drew three rings at 3, 5 and 8 miles. ☐ keep them ☐ change to: ___ , ___ , ___ miles
- **Typical delivery time** to each ring: about ___ / ___ / ___ minutes
- **Delivery fee**: ☐ none ☐ $___ ☐ depends on distance
- **Minimum order**: ☐ none ☐ $___
- **Who delivers**: ☐ our own driver ☐ DoorDash ☐ Uber Eats ☐ our register's online ordering ☐ not sure yet
- **Pickup**: an order is usually ready in about ___ minutes
- **Dozen empanadas**: ☐ same price each ☐ dozen price $___ ; notice needed for big orders: ___
- **Gluten-free** options we can name: ___
- **Reviews**: ☐ Yes, you may reuse the three Google reviews from the market's website on the café site

## Page 4: Photos (phone is fine; we'll match the colors)
A checklist with a one-line "how to shoot it" for each. Daylight, no flash, hold the phone steady, take three of each:
1. **The front door** from the parking lot, sign readable, around 10 am
2. **The counter** from where a customer stands, someone working if they're happy to be in it
3. **Hands crimping an empanada** (the repulgue), close up, from above. The most important photo on the site.
4. **A tray of empanadas** straight from the oven
5. **The lomito** plated, on the real table
6. **The milanesa** plated, on the real table
7. **Today's board**, straight on, readable
8. **Marina at the counter**, only if she's comfortable
Then: "Text them to Kyle at (his number) or AirDrop at the café. Full size, not screenshots."

## Back of the last page: what happens next
Three lines: Kyle types her words into the site exactly as written; the hidden sections appear; she sees it on her phone before anything goes live.
Kyle's contact line. Stringham Web Design.

## Style notes for the PDF
- Letter size, portrait, black text on white for printing, with one accent color per page from the palette above.
- Big input boxes with light ruled lines (at least 5 lines per story box).
- Plain words. No "confirm", no jargon. Spanish accents welcome (Milanesa, Lomito, empanadas).
- Page numbers and "Nature's Garden Cafe & Resto" in the footer.
